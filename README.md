# The Morning After — Friday 20 November 2026 (46 stories)

A one-page newspaper of Friday 20 November 2026: **46 stories** — thirteen
panels with a source line under each, a world map, a stat strip and 33 briefs —
every story happened, or was first reported, on Friday. Items marked "due" are
Friday's scheduled events, labelled as such.

Entry for the make.fun bounty
[news-image-20261120](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261120).

## Why "The Morning After"

Thursday 19 November was enormous — a record IPO debut, a three-power summit, a
genocide ruling, a game launch, Wall Street's sixth straight fall. Friday's
real news was what the world did next: a cyclone ashore with the official
evacuation count (298,676 people, PTI), missiles on Kyiv, a regional market
slump, climate talks outrunning their last day, a UK–EU summit, an Italian
general strike, and 40 more. The page's DATE CHECK strip names Thursday's
giants — including two rival pages' Friday-dated heroes — and keeps them off
the page as news.

## Files

- `index.html` — the front page (self-contained; original inline-SVG art)
- `entry.png` — the rendered page, 1024×1024
- `STORIES.md` — the 46 stories and the source/date check behind each
- `main.py` — copies `entry.png` to the output directory and prints a one-line dossier

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```
