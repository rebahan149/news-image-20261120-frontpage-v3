1 · GOPALPUR · STORM · TODAY

Akvan comes ashore in India

Severe Cyclone Akvan crossed the Andhra–Odisha coast near Gopalpur around noon, winds 70–80 km/h gusting 90. Officials counted 298,676 people evacuated to 2,523 relief camps, 423 rescue teams deployed, some 70 trains cancelled.

Source: IMD via PTI
2 · KYIV · WAR · TODAY

Ballistic missiles rain on Kyiv

Russia struck the capital with ballistic missiles and drones in Friday's early hours. Debris set a three-storey building ablaze in Obolonskyi; fires burned across districts — retaliation, Kyiv said, for the bridge strike.

Source: Reuters
3 · TOKYO · MARKETS · TODAY

Asia's markets slump

The Nikkei fell 4.6% to about 56,698 — down more than 2,700 points at its worst — as AI shares led a regional sell-off. Seoul's Kospi lost 4.8% and Australia's ASX 200 fell 1.1% in Friday dealing.

Source: Reuters / AAP
4 · ANTALYA · CLIMATE · TODAY

COP31 outruns its final day

The summit's last scheduled day arrived with the draft in retreat — every fossil-fuel line struck, the $120bn adaptation figure gone. Needs run to $305bn a year; the tripling goal survives, the details don't.

Source: Reuters / BBC
5 · WELLINGTON · SPORT · TODAY

Phillips makes history with 118

Glenn Phillips' 118 off 216 balls on day two made him the first player to score centuries in all three formats against India in one year. New Zealand reached 317/6; Nicholls 146 not out.

Source: ICC / IANS
6 · TOKYO · ECONOMY · TODAY

Inflation stays over target

Japan's core consumer prices rose 2.2% in October, Friday's data showed — under the 2.3% forecast but above the Bank of Japan's target a second month. The governor signalled a near-term rise.

Source: Reuters
7 · NEW YORK · GAMES · TODAY

GTA VI passes $2.36bn

Sensor Tower's Friday update put revenue past $2.36bn on some 24.6 million copies — in the game's first full day on sale, a day after the launch that tied the all-time Metacritic record at 99.

Source: Sensor Tower via Deadline
8 · NEW DELHI · DIPLOMACY · TODAY

Pakistan minister reaches Delhi

The SCO Civilisational Dialogue opened at Purana Qila, inaugurated by PM Modi. Pakistan's Musadik Malik leads his country's team — the first visit by a Pakistani federal minister since May 2023.

Source: The News / Dainik Jagran
9 · WASHINGTON · POLITICS · LATER TODAY

Trump faces the press at noon

A White House news conference is set for 12:00 ET — the president's first since Shenzhen, with Iran, the declaration and Thursday's market rout all waiting for him at the podium.

Source: White House schedule
10 · BRUSSELS · SUMMIT · TODAY

Britain and the EU, face to face

Burnham met Costa and von der Leyen at a first UK–EU summit in years, a food deal the UK values at £5.1bn a year on the table.

Source: Reuters / GOV.UK
11 · ROME · STRIKE · TODAY

Italy stops for a day

The CGIL's 24-hour general strike hit buses, metros and schools, with some 100 rallies; a rail strike runs into Saturday.

Source: ANSA / ETUC
12 · BEIJING · RATES · TODAY

Loan rates held, 18th month

China's one-year loan prime rate stayed at 3.00% and the five-year at 3.50% at Friday's fixing, as surveyed.

Source: Reuters
13 · FLORIDA · WEATHER · TODAY

Floods keep schools shut

Over a foot of rain in hours kept schools closed across seven counties; 16.65 inches fell near Dania Beach.

Source: CNN
